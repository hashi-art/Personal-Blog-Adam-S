05 / A personal note

Freedom, with direction.

“We live in a time of extraordinary digital connection, where social norms are constantly being challenged and rewritten. For Generation Z, that brings both remarkable freedom of expression and the pressure of a fast-moving, always-connected world. To me, genuine freedom is not simply the absence of rules or the urge to follow every trend. It grows from self-mastery, emotional intelligence, and conscious choices.”

“When instant gratification is celebrated, choosing purpose over peer pressure is a powerful statement. Healthy boundaries and everyday decisions shape the future we build. With an open mind and steady integrity, we can make the most of our generation’s opportunities without losing sight of our values or direction.”

Escape from Freedom (also published as The Fear of Freedom) by Erich Fromm explores the tension between personal freedom, belonging, and the pressure to conform. Its questions about choosing one’s own direction resonate with this perspective.
